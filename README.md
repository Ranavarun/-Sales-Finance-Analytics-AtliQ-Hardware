# -Sales-Finance-Analytics-AtliQ-Hardware
As part of my Sales and Finance Analytics project, I analyzed AtliQ Hardware’s market performance across 2019, 2020, and 2021, leveraging Microsoft Excel, PowerPivot, and Business Intelligence (BI) tools to generate data-driven insights.

🔍 Key Highlights & Insights:
✅ Net Sales Performance Analysis:

Conducted a deep dive into sales trends across years, customers, markets, and products to identify key growth patterns and areas for improvement.
✅ Market Performance & Strategic Alignment:

Compared sales performance against predefined business targets, enabling strategic adjustments to optimize revenue streams.
Uncovered insights into regional and product-specific sales growth, helping refine market strategies.
✅ Operational & Financial Insights:

Identified top-performing product categories and regions, guiding resource allocation for maximum impact.
Analyzed growth metrics to track financial performance and enhance decision-making.
🚀 Impact & Takeaways:
By leveraging data analytics and business intelligence, I transformed raw data into actionable insights to drive strategic business decisions and improve overall financial performance.
